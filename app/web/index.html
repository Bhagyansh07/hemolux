Selective prediction: value of abstaining

Rejecting the lowest quality 15% of images reduces mean absolute error by ~38%. Refusing to estimate is a critical safety feature.

Corpus limitation: zero severe cases

Across all 217 patients (126 normal, 76 mild, 15 moderate), there are ZERO severe anaemia cases (<7.0 g/dL). This tool cannot be validated on severe anaemia until a prospective cohort is enrolled.

Method & limits

How it works

The camera photographs the conjunctiva, the pink lining inside the lower eyelid, which carries the same capillary blood as a finger-prick. A regression model estimates haemoglobin in g/dL from that image. All computation happens on your device.

Light absorption & skin pigmentation

Why smartphone screening is difficult: Haemoglobin and melanin absorb light in overlapping spectral wavelengths (490–577 nm).

ITA: +25° (Intermediate)

Notice how melanin absorbance extends across the blue-green spectrum where haemoglobin Q-bands sit. Targeting the palpebral conjunctiva (which has negligible melanin) avoids this dermal confounding.

What it cannot do

This is a screening aid, not a diagnosis. It cannot confirm anaemia, cannot rule it out, and cannot replace a laboratory blood test. A result outside the normal range is a reason to seek a blood test, not a reason to start treatment.

Do not use it in an emergency or to make a treatment decision.

Fairness

Performance is reported separately by skin tone (ITA°) and by imaging site. Where the model is not equally reliable, that gap is stated rather than averaged away.

Clinical validation protocol (Planned)

Prospective protocol planned with secondary healthcare centers: venous CBC reference, ethical clearance (IEC), minimum 25% deep pigmentation cohort.

  • Reference Standard: Automated Coulter counter / Sysmex venous complete blood count within 30 minutes of photograph.
  • Ethics: Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) clearance with written informed consent.
  • Subgroup Target: Enrolling ≥25% individuals with ITA° < 10° (Fitzpatrick V–VI) to ensure statistical power for C3 gate.
  • Clinician Advisory: Collaborative protocol review with registered hematologists.
